Emergency Priority Score (PS)

PS = 0.35S + 0.25P + 0.20W + 0.10R + 0.10D
Weighted Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) for Triaging
  • S (0.35): Severity of Incident Scaled 1-10
  • P (0.25): Population Affected Scaled 0-10
  • W (0.20): Elapsed Waiting Time Hours waiting
  • R (0.10): Resource Shortage Unmet supply gap
  • D (0.10): Distance from Help Isolation factor
Classification Thresholds:
Critical ≥ 7.5
High 5.5-7.4
Medium 3.5-5.4
Low < 3.5
